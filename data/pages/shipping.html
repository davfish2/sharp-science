Shipping

Where we deliver

We deliver within Australia only. We do not ship overseas.

Carrier and dispatch

Orders ship by Australia Post Express Post. We dispatch daily. Orders placed before 12pm AWST are dispatched the same day.

Orders placed after the cut-off are dispatched the next dispatch day.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on orders over $200 AUD. Prices include GST. Shipping for smaller orders is calculated at checkout.

Note for client: the cut-off time and free shipping threshold can be changed. Please confirm both.

Delivery times

Delivery times depend on Australia Post and your location. Remote areas may take longer. You will receive tracking details when your order ships.

Damaged or missing parcels

Please check your parcel on arrival. If glassware arrives damaged, contact us at [Email] with your order number and photos. See the returns page for next steps.
